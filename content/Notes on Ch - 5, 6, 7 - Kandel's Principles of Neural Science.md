# 4a

### **Main Ideas**

#### **1. Genomic Scale of Ion Channel Diversity**

- Human genome encodes:
    
    - 9 voltage-gated Na⁺ channels
        
    - 10 Ca²⁺ channels
        
    - > 75 K⁺ channels
        
    - ~70 ligand-gated channels
        
    - > 12 Cl⁻ channels
        
- Despite this diversity, most channels fall into **a few gene superfamilies**.
    

#### **2. Gene Superfamilies and Evolution**

- Channels within a superfamily share:
    
    - Similar amino acid sequences
        
    - Common transmembrane topology
        
    - Related physiological roles
        
- All superfamilies evolved via **gene duplication and divergence** from ancestral genes.
    

#### **3. Ligand-Gated Ion Channel Superfamily (Cys-loop receptors)**

- Includes receptors for **ACh, GABA, glycine, serotonin**.
    
- Structure:
    
    - 5 subunits per channel
        
    - Each subunit has **4 transmembrane α-helices**
        
    - Conserved **13–amino acid cysteine loop** with a disulfide bond
        
- Classified by **agonist type** and **ion selectivity**.
    
- **Glutamate receptors** belong to a **separate gene family**.
    

#### **4. Gap Junction Channels**

- Form electrical synapses between cells.
    
- Structure:
    
    - 12 identical subunits
        
    - Each subunit has **4 transmembrane segments**
        
- Encoded by a distinct gene superfamily.
    

#### **5. Voltage-Gated Ion Channels**

- Generate action potentials; selective for **Na⁺, Ca²⁺, or K⁺**.
    
- Core structure:
    
    - **Six transmembrane segments (S1–S6)**
        
    - Conserved **P-region** between S5–S6 forms the selectivity filter
        
- Architecture:
    
    - Na⁺ and Ca²⁺ channels → **single large subunit with 4 repeats**
        
    - K⁺ channels → **four separate subunits**, each with one repeat
        

#### **6. K⁺ Channel Families**

- **Voltage-gated K⁺ channels** (major family)
    
- **Inward-rectifier K⁺ channels**:
    
    - Open at resting potential
        
    - Close on depolarization
        
    - Only **2 transmembrane segments + P-region**
        
- **Two-pore-domain K⁺ channels**:
    
    - Two repeated pore-forming segments
        
    - Contribute to resting K⁺ conductance
        

#### **7. TRP Channels and Other Families**

- **TRP channels**:
    
    - Nonselective cation channels
        
    - 6 transmembrane segments + P-region
        
    - Gated mainly by intracellular ligands
        
    - Roles: Ca²⁺ homeostasis, pain, temperature, vision, mechanosensation, taste
        
- Other families include:
    
    - Cl⁻ channels regulating resting membrane potential
        
    - ATP-gated ligand-gated cation channels
        

#### **8. Mechanisms Generating Further Diversity**

- **Heteromultimeric assembly** of subunits
    
- **Alternative mRNA splicing**
    
- **RNA editing**
    
- Differential expression across:
    
    - Developmental stages
        
    - Cell types
        
    - Subcellular regions
        

#### **9. Functional and Clinical Significance**

- Structural micro-variations enable precise neuronal functions.
    
- Channel diversity offers the potential for **highly selective drugs** targeting specific brain regions with minimal side effects.
